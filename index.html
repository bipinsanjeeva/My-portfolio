ABOUT ME

COPYWRITING PRINCIPLE
Every word needs a reason. The strategy is hidden behind the sentence. Don't guess. Find the reason they buy.

I'm Bipin Sanjeeva, an Email Copywriter focused on eCommerce brands.

I believe effective copy starts long before writing the first sentence. I research the brand, understand the audience, identify what customers want, and use those insights to create emails that feel relevant, persuasive, and true to the brand.

My focus is helping eCommerce brands communicate clearly, build stronger relationships with their customers, and turn attention into action.
